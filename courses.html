Our Courses & Syllabus Breakdown

1. Noorani Qaida for Beginners

The Noorani Qaida is the gold-standard stepping stone for every child and beginner looking to read the Holy Quran with accurate Arabic phonetics. Our tutors use digital interactive tools, colorful illustrations, and repetitive drills to ensure the student grasps each letter's articulation point (Makhraj).

Detailed Curriculum Modules:

Lesson 1-4: Individual Arabic Alphabets, Shapes & Joining Letters
Lesson 5-8: Harakaat (Fatha, Kasra, Damma) & Tanween (Double Vowels)
Lesson 9-12: Sukoon, Jazm, and Letters of Madd & Leen
Lesson 13-17: Tashdeed (Shaddah), Nunation, and Final Fluency Drills

2. Quran Reading with Tajweed Rules

Tajweed literally means "proficiency" and "beautification". Allah commanded in Surah Al-Muzzammil: "And recite the Quran with measured recitation." In this course, students learn to recite the Holy Quran with melodious tone, applying every rule of articulation and phonetic precision.

Core Learning Objectives:

Rules of Noon Sakinah & Tanween (Izhar, Idgham, Iqlab, Ikhfa)
Rules of Meem Sakinah (Ikhfa Shafawi, Idgham Shafawi, Izhar Shafawi)
Qalqalah (Echoing sound), Heavy & Light Letters (Tafkheem & Tarqeeq)
Stopping Signs (Ahkam-e-Waqf) & Breath Control Management

Ijaza Certified Mentors

Taught by reciters holding certified Asaneed (chains of transmission) back to the Holy Prophet ﷺ.

3. Quran Memorization Program (Hifz)

Memorizing the Holy Quran is one of the highest honors for any believer. The Prophet ﷺ said: "The best among you are those who learn the Quran and teach it." Halenoor Academy provides a structured, stress-free Hifz environment following the traditional Madrasa tri-part system.

The 3-Pillar Daily System:

1. Sabaq (New Lesson) Daily new verses memorized and recited to the teacher with zero errors.
2. Sabqi (Recent Work) Daily revision of the last 5-10 pages to lock recent memory into long-term storage.
3. Manzil (Old Revision) Systematic daily revision of previously completed Juz so nothing is ever forgotten.

Huffaz Mentors

Guided exclusively by teachers who have memorized the full Quran and have at least 5 years of coaching experience.

Formal Sanad / Hifz Certificate
