Privacy & Child Protection Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Halenoor Quran Academy

1. Our Commitment to Student Privacy

At Halenoor Quran Academy (ہماری ہالہ نور آن لائن قران اکیڈمی), protecting your family's personal information is our sacred trust. We are deeply committed to respecting the privacy of our students, parents, and tutors globally. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, and protect your information when using our website and enrolled in our classes.

2. Information We Collect

We only gather information necessary to conduct your 1-on-1 Quran classes and handle scheduling:

  • Guardian & Student Details: Name, age, gender (to assign matching male/female teachers), preferred language, and academic level.
  • Contact Details: WhatsApp phone number and email address for class scheduling and parent-teacher updates.
  • Timezone & Availability: Country and preferred time slots to ensure accurate scheduling with tutors.
  • No Financial Data Stored: We do not store credit card or bank credentials on our web servers.

3. Children's Online Privacy & Video Etiquette

Since many of our students are young children, we enforce rigorous child protection rules:

  • Parents and guardians are encouraged to sit in or monitor their child's live classes at any time.
  • Classes are conducted via secure, password-protected Zoom or Skype links provided exclusively to the parent.
  • Tutors are strictly forbidden from contacting students outside authorized class schedules or asking for personal social media handles.
  • No audio/video recordings of student recitations are made without express prior consent from parents.

4. Sisters Program & Female Teacher Privacy

For female students and sisters enrolled in our Sisters Special Program, classes are taught exclusively by certified female scholars in a private, respectful, and safe digital Islamic environment.
