Academy Terms

Terms of Service & Class Policies

Please read these simple guidelines that govern our educational services.

1. 3-Day Free Trial Policy

Halenoor Quran Academy provides 3 consecutive trial sessions completely free of charge. No debit or credit card is required to register. After the trial is concluded, parents decide freely whether to enroll in regular monthly classes. There is zero obligation to continue.

2. Attendance & Rescheduling Classes

  • Class Punctuality: Both tutors and students must join the scheduled session promptly at the agreed time slot.
  • Rescheduling: If a student cannot attend a session due to illness, school exams, or travel, parents must inform the coordinator or tutor at least 4 hours in advance so a makeup class can be scheduled.
  • Tutor Absences: In the rare event of tutor sickness or technical connectivity disruptions, a makeup class will be arranged at your convenience or covered by a substitute certified teacher.

3. Fee Payments & Family Discounts

  • Tuition fees are billed monthly in advance after the completion of the 3-day free trial.
  • We offer flexible multi-currency payments via Bank Transfer, PayPal, Wise, Western Union, and local bank accounts.
  • Family Discount: A 10% discount applies when enrolling 2 siblings, and a 15% discount applies when enrolling 3 or more siblings.
  • There are zero admission fees and zero hidden software charges.

4. Code of Conduct & Islamic Adab

The Quran is the sacred Speech of Allah Almighty. Both our esteemed teachers and our beloved students are expected to maintain the highest Islamic adab (etiquette), cleanliness, respectful dress code, and attentive listening during the holy recitation sessions.

5. Questions or Support

Our Academic Coordinator desk is available 24/7 on WhatsApp for schedule adjustments, teacher switches, or curriculum queries.
